Curriculum Architecture

Math Course Outline (Levels 1–12)

Progressive mastery from elementary number sense to complex numbers, polar coordinates, and De Moivre's Theorem.

Connecting session...
Level 1 • Grade 1
Arithmetic Foundations
  • Counting, number recognition, and place value to 100
  • Addition and subtraction within 20 with strategies
  • Basic shapes, patterns, and sorting
  • Simple measurement (length, time, money)
  • Introduction to data (tally marks, simple graphs)
Level 2 • Grade 2
Place Value & Operations
  • Place value to 1,000; skip-counting
  • Addition & subtraction within 100 with regrouping
  • Intro to multiplication concepts (arrays, equal groups)
  • Measurement (length, time, money) & simple data
  • 2D and 3D shapes and geometric attributes
Level 3 • Grade 3
Fractions & Multiplicative Thinking
  • Multiplication and division facts and strategies
  • Fractions as parts of a whole and on a number line
  • Area and perimeter of rectangles
  • Time, capacity, and mass units
  • Intro to data representation and probability
Level 4 • Grade 4
Multi-Digit Arithmetic & Decimals
  • Multi-digit multiplication and long division
  • Equivalent fractions, comparisons, mixed numbers
  • Decimals in tenths and hundredths
  • Factors, multiples, prime & composite numbers
  • Angle concepts, shape classification & conversions
Level 5 • Grade 5
Rational Arithmetic & Coordinate Plane
  • Operations with multi-digit numbers and decimals
  • Adding, subtracting, multiplying, dividing fractions
  • Volume of rectangular prisms
  • Coordinate plane (Quadrant 1) and point plotting
  • Order of operations and algebraic expressions
Level 6 • Grade 6
Ratios & Proportional Reasoning
  • Ratios, unit rates, and proportional reasoning
  • Percentages and conversions (fractions ↔ decimals ↔ percents)
  • Integers and the full four-quadrant coordinate plane
  • Expressions, equations, and inequalities
  • Area of triangles/parallelograms & surface area/volume
  • Statistical measures (mean, median, mode, range)
Level 7 • Grade 7
Rational Equations & Geometry
  • Proportional relationships and constant of proportionality
  • Operations with rational numbers and negative integers
  • Solving multi-step equations and inequalities
  • Scale drawings, circles (circumference & area), angles
  • Theoretical/experimental probability and sampling
Level 8 • Grade 8
Linear Functions & Pythagorean Geometry
  • Linear equations, systems of linear equations, and functions
  • Slope, intercepts, and graphing linear relationships
  • Pythagorean Theorem and coordinate distance formula
  • Transformations, congruence, and similarity
  • Volume of cylinders, cones, and spheres
  • Scientific notation, exponent laws, and square roots
Level 9 • Grade 9 (Algebra 1)
Algebraic Modeling & Quadratics
  • Deep dive into linear functions, systems, and inequalities
  • Quadratic functions (graphing, factoring, completing the square)
  • Exponential growth and decay functions
  • Polynomials and rational expressions
  • Bivariate statistics, scatter plots, and lines of best fit
  • Function notation and transformations
Level 10 • Grade 10 (Geometry)
Deductive Proofs & Trigonometry
  • Deductive reasoning, formal proofs, and constructions
  • Properties of triangles, congruence, and similarity
  • Right-triangle trigonometry (sine, cosine, tangent)
  • Circle theorems, equations, arcs, and sectors
  • Coordinate geometry proofs and analytical vectors
Level 11 • Grade 11 (Algebra 2 / Precalc)
Advanced Functions & Unit Circle
  • Advanced functions (polynomial, rational, radical, absolute value)
  • Exponential and logarithmic functions
  • Sequences, series, and arithmetic/geometric progressions
  • Trigonometric functions (unit circle, identities, equations)
  • Complex numbers (basic operations & polar form intro)
  • Nonlinear systems, matrices, and conic sections
Level 12 • Grade 12 Masterclass Precalculus & Advanced Topics

Polar Coordinates, Parametric Equations & De Moivre’s Theorem

Level 12 synthesizes arithmetic, algebra, and trigonometry into sophisticated mathematical frameworks required for advanced calculus, physics, and engineering.

1. De Moivre’s Theorem

Computes powers and roots of complex numbers efficiently in polar form: zn = rn [cos(nθ) + i sin(nθ)]. Used to derive multiple-angle trigonometric identities and solve cyclotomic equations.

2. Polar & Parametric Curves

Graphing cardiods, roses, and spirals in polar coordinates. Modeling projectile motion and complex mechanical trajectories using parametric equations in 2D and 3D space.

3. Calculus Bridge & Engineering

Connecting Euler’s formula (eiθ = cos θ + i sin θ) to AC circuit phasor analysis, signal processing, Fourier transforms, and introductory limits/derivatives.
