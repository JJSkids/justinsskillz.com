📖 Lesson 1: Articles & Noun Gender Rules

Gender & Definite/Indefinite Articles

Every noun in French possesses a grammatical gender (masculine or feminine). Articles must always match the gender and number of the noun:

  • Definite ("The"): le (masculine), la (feminine), l' (before vowels/silent h), les (plural).
  • Indefinite ("A / Some"): un (masculine), une (feminine), des (plural).

The 5 French Diacritical Marks

Accents modify vowel sounds or distinguish homonyms.

Accent Aigu

è / à / ù

Accent Grave

ê / î / ô

Circonflexe

Cédille

Turns 'c' into a soft "s" sound before a, o, u.
