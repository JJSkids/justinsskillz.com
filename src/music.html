🎼 Lesson 1: How to Read Notes on the Treble Clef

The Musical Staff

Music is written on a staff consisting of 5 horizontal lines and 4 spaces between them. The Treble Clef (𝄔) indicates notes above Middle C.

  • Lines (Bottom to Top): E - G - B - D - F (Mnemonic: "Every Good Boy Does Fine")
  • Spaces (Bottom to Top): F - A - C - E (Spells "FACE")

🎹 Lesson 2: Constructing Major & Minor Chords

Understanding Triads & Intervals

A triad is a 3-note chord built using semitones (half steps between neighboring piano keys).

  • Major Triad: Root + 4 Semitones (Major 3rd) + 3 Semitones (Minor 3rd). Sound: Bright / Happy.
  • Minor Triad: Root + 3 Semitones (Minor 3rd) + 4 Semitones (Major 3rd). Sound: Melancholy / Sad.

🌊 Lesson 4: Sound Waves & Frequency physics

Frequencies and Pitch

Pitch is determined by the frequency (measured in Hertz, Hz) of vibrating sound waves. Standard concert pitch sets A4 = 440 Hz. Doubling the frequency raises the pitch by exactly one Octave (e.g., A5 = 880 Hz).
